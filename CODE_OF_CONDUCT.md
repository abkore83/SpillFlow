# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Disagreement about designs and
tradeoffs is expected; harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct harms the project or its community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
